Enzo The Elephant

Teaching Language Through Music

An estimated 41% of the forcibly displaced people in the world are children. Language is one of the primary changes a child must process. Communication becomes increasingly difficult, and children feel alienated.

An effective tool to learn a language quickly is to incorporate music. Enzo helps children learn new words by correlating them to music. By using generative audio programs, the device can create unique melodies.
